Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use hanjo.ai, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, screen magnification, or other assistive technology. Last updated: November 2026.

1. Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA. This is ongoing work, and we check the Site again whenever we change it.

2. What we have done

Text and buttons meet AA color contrast. Every page can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a link that skips to the main content. Form fields have labels, and images have text alternatives. Pages hold up at 200% zoom and on small screens, and animations stop when your device is set to reduce motion. The English and Japanese versions follow the same standard.

3. How we check

We test the Site with automated accessibility tools and by using it with a keyboard. We have not yet completed a full review with screen reader users, so some issues may remain.

4. Known limitations

Examples of our client work, such as emails and website screenshots, are shown as images. Their text alternatives describe what each image shows but do not reproduce every word in it.

5. Tell us about a problem

If anything on the Site is hard to use, or you need information in another format, please let us know through our contact page. Tell us which page and what went wrong, and we will reply within two business days and work on a fix.
