What founders usually ask first.

Do you write the copy and design the emails?

Yes, full-service. We write all copy and design every email. You supply your brand assets (logo, product photos, brand guidelines) and we handle the rest end-to-end.

What does it cost?

Projects typically start from $1,500 – $3,000 depending on scope. You'll get a clear number on the call. No vague proposals, no surprises.

How long does a typical project take?

A full flow build typically takes 3–5 weeks from kickoff to launch. An audit takes about one week. Timelines depend on scope and how quickly you can provide feedback and brand assets.

Which email platform will we use?

Whichever fits your stage and stack. We set it up, or migrate you over, and run it. If you're not ready, we'll tell you what to do first so you don't waste a setup.

Generic email setups don't account for that. We build ones that do.
